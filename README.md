# Quick-Commerce Ad Campaign Performance & Attribution Dashboard

## 📌 Overview

The Quick-Commerce Ad Campaign Performance & Attribution Dashboard is an end-to-end marketing analytics project designed to measure the effectiveness of digital advertising campaigns in the quick-commerce industry.

The project analyzes campaign data across channels, campaigns, customer segments, and products to understand where advertising spend generates the highest engagement, conversions, and revenue. An interactive dashboard transforms raw campaign data into actionable insights for evaluating marketing performance and optimizing advertising investment.

## 🎯 Business Problem

Quick-commerce businesses run advertising campaigns across multiple channels, but high ad spend does not always translate into high conversions or revenue.

This project addresses key questions such as:

- Which advertising channels generate the highest conversions?
- Which campaigns provide the best return on advertising spend?
- Where are customers dropping off in the conversion funnel?
- Which campaigns have high spending but poor performance?
- Which customer segments respond best to advertising?
- Which channels contribute most to conversions?

## 📊 Key KPIs

The dashboard tracks:

- Impressions
- Clicks
- Click-Through Rate (CTR)
- Ad Spend
- Conversions
- Conversion Rate
- Cost Per Click (CPC)
- Cost Per Acquisition (CPA)
- Revenue
- Return on Ad Spend (ROAS)
- Customer Attribution

## 🔍 Key Analysis

### Campaign Performance
Compare campaigns based on spend, conversions, revenue, and ROAS to identify high- and low-performing campaigns.

### Channel Analysis
Evaluate the effectiveness of different advertising channels and determine which channels generate the strongest returns.

### Conversion Funnel
Analyze the customer journey from impressions → clicks → conversions to identify major drop-off points.

### Attribution Analysis
Measure the contribution of different marketing touchpoints toward customer conversions.

### Customer Segmentation
Compare campaign performance across different customer segments to identify high-value audiences.
